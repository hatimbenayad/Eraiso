Planned Functional Work Breakdown (CU01–CU09 & CU20–CU26)

  • Patient Portal Workflows (RF01–RF09):
    Patient requests radiological appointment (CU01), consults scheduled appointments (CU02), requests soft cancellation (CU03), consults published diagnostic reports (CU04), and views DICOM images (CU05).
  • Admissions & Reception Personnel (RF20–RF23):
    Inpatient/outpatient clinical admission check-in (CU20), calendar schedule allocation for radiodiagnostic rooms (CU21), and schedule modification/reassignment (CU22).
  • Billing, Invoicing & Incident Auditing (RF24–RF26):
    Calculate procedure fees and generate invoices (CU24), record insurance payment coverage (CU25), and admissions incident registration (CU26).
  • Notification Dispatcher Engine (CU07, CU26):
    Automated notification service alerting patients of confirmed appointment dates and notifying physicians of emergency findings.

Architectural Dependency Rationale (Why It3 Follows It2)

In the Unified Process, construction order is strictly governed by entity and operational dependencies:

  • Orders Precede Appointments: A Patient or Admissions clerk cannot schedule an appointment (CU01 / CU21) unless a validated ExaminationOrder with procedure modality and priority exists (built in It2).
  • Reports Precede Patient Access: A Patient cannot view clinical findings or images (CU04 / CU05) until the Physician diagnostic report service and PACS adapter (built in It2) are active.
  • Shared Contracts: Iteration 3 directly reuses the AuthSession RBAC tokens, in-memory repository architecture (D-030), and Mock Adapters (D-012) proven in Iteration 2.
Transition Gate: Completing It3 unlocks the final Transition Phase (It4 / ItN), where all 32 use cases undergo end-to-end acceptance testing for the Dec 15 delivery.
