Problem 1: Parking Fee Calculator (ET.02.02)

Repo: ISO2-2025-GrupoA05-Testing-P1

Calculate total parking stay cost based on entry and exit Date/Time ticket objects.

  • Encapsulation & Validation: If exitTime < entryTime or if null objects are passed, throw an appropriate custom exception. Implement validations at constructor level with full encapsulation.
  • Pricing Rules:
    • First 30 minutes: completely free (€0.00).
    • Minute 31 onward: charged at €0.05 per minute.
    • Daily ceiling: capped at a strict maximum limit of €20.00 per day.
  • Architecture: Domain logic decoupled from UI/CLI.

Problem 2: University Scholarship Eligibility (ET.02.03)

Repo: ISO2-2025-GrupoA05-Testing-P2

Determine the single matching scholarship category for a student based on: GPA (0–10), Family Income (€), Distance (km), Disability (%), and Siblings.

  • 1. Excellence Scholarship: GPA ≥ 8.5 AND Family Income < €30,000 → Full tuition waiver + €1,000 stipend.
  • 2. Residence Scholarship: GPA between 6.5 and 8.4 AND Family Income < €20,000 AND Distance > 50 km → Full tuition waiver + housing allowance.
  • 3. Large Family Scholarship: GPA ≥ 5.0 AND Family Income strictly < €15,000 AND Siblings ≥ 3 → Full tuition waiver.
  • 4. Inclusion Scholarship: Disability ≥ 33% AND GPA ≥ 5.0 → Full tuition waiver + assistive technology funding.
  • Zero-Ambiguity Rule: No student may receive conflicting awards; explicit boundary conditions and exceptions must be documented.

Problem 3: Agricultural Smart Greenhouse Irrigation (ET.02.04)

Repo: ISO2-2025-GrupoA05-Testing-P3

Automated irrigation decision engine based on: Soil Moisture (%), Ambient Temperature (°C), Rain Forecast (boolean/imminent), and Time of Day (HH:mm).

  • Heavy Watering: Moisture < 20% AND Temp > 25°C AND No rain forecasted within next 24 hours.
  • Light Watering: Moisture between 20% and 40% AND Temp > 25°C AND No rain forecasted.
  • Do Not Water: Moisture < 40% AND imminent rain is forecasted.
  • Emergency Drainage: Moisture > 60% AND Temp < 5°C (prevents root freeze damage).
  • Peak Solar Radiation Rule (12:00 PM – 4:00 PM): All standard watering postponed, UNLESS Moisture drops below 10%, in which case Critical Watering must execute.
